Common questions

For learners

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free and unlimited while we're in beta. If we introduce paid plans later, beta testers will hear first.

Which grades does it cover?

Grade 12 (Matric) right now. Grade 10 and 11 are coming soon — the tutoring already handles their questions, we're just adding dedicated content.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It runs entirely inside WhatsApp on any phone — no app, no new login, no data-heavy downloads.

Can it help in Afrikaans?

Yes. It tutors across all 11 CAPS subjects including Afrikaans Huistaal, and will answer in Afrikaans.

What if it gets something wrong?

It's grounded in the official CAPS curriculum, but it's still AI — always sanity-check important answers against your textbook or teacher, and tell us when something looks off.

For parents

Is this just a way for my child to cheat?

The opposite. It's built to coach, not hand over answers — it uses step-by-step questioning and a “hint mode” that nudges without solving, so your child builds the skill themselves.

Is it safe and appropriate for my child?

It stays strictly on school subjects and redirects anything off-topic. We keep only what's needed to run the tutor.

What will it cost after the beta?

It's free now. We'll share pricing before any paid plans launch, and there'll be a genuinely useful free option.

For teachers & schools

Will this replace teachers?

No — it's an after-hours study aid that extends your teachers, never replaces them. It's strongest as homework and revision support between lessons.

Is it aligned to what we actually teach?

Yes. Answers are checked against official CAPS curriculum documents and NSC exam conventions (mark allocations, command words, method marks).

Does it work for IEB schools?

Yes for content (IEB and NSC share CAPS). Past papers and exam technique are NSC-based today; full IEB exam support (papers, rubrics, the annual theme) is on the way, and the tutor is honest with learners about that distinction.

How do we pilot it as a school?

Schools can join the free beta as a founding partner — a small pilot group and light feedback over a term. Email hello@studymatesa.co.za.
